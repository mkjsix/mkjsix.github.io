Mobility · National traffic information

Replacing an inherited traffic information system

I assessed the inherited PHP and Oracle system for a national traffic agency: it could not scale and went down repeatedly. I designed its replacement: 18 services on AWS that merge six sources on DATEX II, GTFS and TMC every 3 to 5 minutes and serve about 10,000 events a day to HERE, TomTom, Radio24 and others.

Public sector · National compliance register

Locating the load on a register of 400,000 business sites

54% of database waits came from writing a log row for every API request: 2 billion rows, 13 TB. Caching token validation cut total SQL time by 77% and database CPU by 89% in load tests. I led the architecture review and the caching strategy with Redis on top of the database.

216×query time, from 325 s to 1.5 s
Retail · Fiscal and EDI documents

Moving 150 million documents off SQL Server

12 servers processed 6,000 files an hour, but searches took minutes and history was limited to two months. MongoDB won a competitive PoC against SQL Server and Oracle. In the design benchmark, the new write path sustained 537 writes per second against the 300 required for ten times the volume.

HR tech · Behavioural assessment

Moving a scoring method out of Excel

A behavioural assessment method, which scores candidates against role profiles, lived in a spreadsheet with its algorithms and 7,500 past responses. I rewrote the scoring engine in Java on RESTHeart and MongoDB, with tests that compare profiles and population statistics against the spreadsheet. The multi-tenant web application reached production in under three months.

AI-assisted development needs engineering discipline

AI makes it possible to build software faster. It doesn't eliminate the need for sound architecture, explicit specifications, or rigorous testing.

I structure AI-assisted development around well-defined tasks, clear boundaries, and continuous validation.

The goal is to increase delivery capacity without sacrificing maintainability, reliability, or control over the system.
